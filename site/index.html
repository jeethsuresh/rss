RSS Reader

A local-first reader for feeds, saved pages, sports, and light AI assist — data stays on your machine.

Download GitHub

Feeds

Subscribe to RSS and Atom sources, refresh on a schedule, and move through articles with keyboard shortcuts. Star, mark read, and open originals in your browser — without an account.

Read Later

Save URLs into a dedicated queue, archive when you’re done, and drop links onto the dock or window. Capture pages to revisit without losing the feed timeline.

Sports

Follow MLB teams with schedules, standings, and game detail. Track F1 weekends across practice, qualifying, and race sessions with classification and championship views.

AI assist

Queue crawl and triage jobs against saved pages. Settings hold your keys and preferences; the queue persists locally and resumes after restart.
